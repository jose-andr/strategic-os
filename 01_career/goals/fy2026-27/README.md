## Portfolio structure

The FY2026/27 goal portfolio includes:

1. Business Goal 1 — Create a cohesive set of CX Strategy tools
2. Business Goal 2 — Build strong customer and business information foundations to enable great customer experiences
3. Business Goal 3 — To be confirmed
4. Development Goal — Develop AI-agentic strategic design practices to augment complex CX work and build team capability
5. Context Goal — Live our ICARE values
6. Context Goal — Make our day-to-day work better

## How to use this folder

Use the portfolio steering plan to understand the full goal system.

Use each dedicated goal file to steer work, identify evidence and clarify the strategic contribution of that goal.

Use the evidence log throughout the year to capture safe examples of progress, decision clarity, stakeholder alignment, team enablement and career proof points.

Use the quarterly review template in `10_templates/` to prepare Culture Amp updates and EOFY performance reflections.

## Quarterly review rhythm

At the end of each quarter:

1. Review progress against each Culture Amp goal.
2. Identify the strongest evidence created during the quarter.
3. Capture safe summaries in the evidence log.
4. Update Culture Amp with concise progress language.
5. Identify the next quarter’s steering priorities.
6. Record any reusable artefacts, prompts, patterns or lessons learned in the appropriate Strategic OS area.

## Strategic intent

Use these goals to direct effort toward work that:

- improves decision clarity
- strengthens customer-centred strategy practice
- builds reusable CX tools
- improves customer and business information foundations
- supports connected customer experiences
- enables team members to adopt and support the work
- demonstrates responsible AI-enabled strategic design practice
- creates evidence for career progression

## Desired reputation

This portfolio supports the Strategic OS desired reputation:

> He helps us make better strategic decisions.

## Source-of-truth rule

Culture Amp remains the formal source of truth for goal status.

Power BI, Service Account dashboards, CRM, Databricks, SharePoint, Jira, Confluence, Teams and other organisational platforms remain the systems of record for operational information and original working material.

Strategic OS should only store:

- safe summaries
- caveats
- source rules
- metric definitions
- interpretation
- reusable prompts
- decision logic
- stakeholder patterns
- lessons learned
- career evidence

Do not store raw organisational data, customer records, dashboard extracts, sensitive operational details, credentials, access tokens, identifiable case examples or unredacted screenshots in Strategic OS.
