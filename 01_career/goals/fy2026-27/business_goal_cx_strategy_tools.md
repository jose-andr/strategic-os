## Culture Amp goal summary

We will lead the next phase of the organisation’s customer-centric transformation by bringing together CX Segments and Modes, Channel Strategy and the CX Roadmap into a cohesive set of tools and practices.

The goal is to enable the Customer Experience team and the broader organisation to consistently put customers at the centre of decision-making.

## Key result areas

1. Deliver and embed the Organisational Customer Strategy
2. Define the point of view on a connected channel experience
3. Maintain and evolve the organisational CX Roadmap

## Strategic OS interpretation

This goal is about turning separate CX strategy artefacts into a practical organisational decision-support system.

The strategic opportunity is to make customer strategy easier to understand, easier to apply and more useful for business planning, prioritisation and investment decisions.

This goal should help shift the work from:

    Separate CX artefacts and conversations
            ↓
    Cohesive CX strategy tools that support customer-centred decisions

## Key activities from Culture Amp

### Deliver and embed the Organisational Customer Strategy

Activities include:

- deliver the Customer Strategy, including Customer Segments, Channel Strategy and CX Roadmap, to inform the broader organisational strategy
- partner with the business to deliver a focused Customer Strategy project that translates customer needs and insights into organisational priorities
- design and deliver an adoption program that builds customer understanding and embeds customer-centric practices across the organisation
- maintain and evolve customer profiles as a living CX resource
- deliver Year 1 of the CX Strategy, with a focus on the New Resident Experience

### Define the point of view on a connected channel experience

Activities include:

- establish a clear Channel Vision and set of principles for a connected customer experience
- define the role of each Mode of Interaction across the customer journey
- provide clear recommendations to guide channel investment and decision-making

### Maintain and evolve the CX Roadmap

Activities include:

- maintain a prioritised view of CX projects and goals that guides business decision-making and investment
- facilitate and deliver quarterly CX Roadmap sessions with the business to review priorities, identify opportunities and continuously refine the roadmap

## Strategic steering questions

Use these during quarterly review:

1. Which CX strategy tools progressed this quarter?
2. What decision, behaviour or organisational conversation did they help improve?
3. Who needed to understand, use or adopt the tools?
4. Were the tools clear enough for others to use without me explaining them every time?
5. What evidence shows the tools influenced business thinking, prioritisation or investment?
6. What was simplified, socialised or reused?
7. What does this work reveal about how the organisation makes customer-centred decisions?
8. What should be captured as career evidence?
9. What needs to be prioritised next quarter?

## Decision-support lens

For each major artefact or activity, clarify:

- What decision does this support?
- Who is the decision-maker or audience?
- What customer evidence or insight is being used?
- What assumption needs to be made visible?
- What trade-off needs to be explained?
- What recommendation is being shaped?
- What action should follow?

## Stakeholder alignment lens

Use this goal to improve how people are brought along the journey.

For each major stakeholder group, consider:

| Stakeholder group | What they need from this goal |
|---|---|
| CX team | Shared language, tools and confidence to explain customer strategy |
| Business stakeholders | Clearer customer priorities and practical decision tools |
| Leaders | Simple narrative connecting customer strategy to organisational strategy |
| Delivery teams | Roadmap clarity and prioritised opportunities |
| Service owners | Customer-centred framing for service improvement decisions |

## Quarterly review rhythm

### Q1

Focus:

- clarify the role of each CX Strategy tool
- identify priority artefacts for the year
- align with Channel Strategy Year 2 and CX Roadmap needs
- identify where adoption support is needed

Evidence to capture:

- baseline artefact map
- priority tools
- early stakeholder feedback
- initial adoption or communication needs

### Q2

Focus:

- refine and socialise key tools
- support connected channel experience framing
- contribute to roadmap conversations
- identify reusable patterns

Evidence to capture:

- updated tools
- channel principles or recommendations
- roadmap session outputs
- decision examples

### Q3

Focus:

- strengthen adoption
- test whether tools are being used by others
- refine artefacts based on stakeholder feedback
- prepare EOFY evidence

Evidence to capture:

- examples of reuse
- stakeholder feedback
- improved roadmap logic
- evidence of clearer customer-centred decision-making

### Q4

Focus:

- consolidate evidence
- identify what became useful and reusable
- prepare performance and career narrative
- recommend next-year improvements

Evidence to capture:

- final artefact summary
- decisions influenced
- adoption evidence
- lessons learned
- career proof points

- what tools were created or improved
