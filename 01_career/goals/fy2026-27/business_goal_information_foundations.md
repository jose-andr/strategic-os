## Culture Amp goal summary

We will strengthen our data, information and knowledge foundations so customers can access consistent, accurate and connected information across channels.

The goal is to establish the frameworks, practices and capabilities needed to deliver continuity of service and create better customer and employee experiences.

## Key result areas

1. Strengthen Information Architecture
2. Improve Customer Account Management
3. Establish a Knowledge-Centred Service approach

## Strategic OS interpretation

This goal is about making connected customer experiences possible by improving the quality, structure and governance of the information that sits underneath them.

The strategic opportunity is to connect information architecture, account management and knowledge management into a coherent service foundation rather than treating them as separate improvement activities.

This goal should help shift the work from:

    Separate information, account and knowledge improvement activities
            ↓
    Connected customer and business information foundations that enable better experiences

## Key activities from Culture Amp

### Strengthen our Information Architecture

Activities include:

- define and establish an improved Information Architecture framework aligned to the scope outlined in Channel Strategy Year 2 work
- design and deliver the Intelligent Front Door, creating a simple, consistent and connected way for customers to find and access information and services

### Improve Customer Account Management

Activities include:

- playback and build on the discovery work completed to date
- identify and prioritise the four most significant immediate account management pain points
- define and deliver standardised approaches to key account management processes across the business
- create a Customer Account Management roadmap that identifies priority business and technical improvements and builds alignment on how they will be addressed

### Establish a Knowledge-Centred Service approach

Activities include:

- explore the value of Knowledge-Centred Service and how it can improve customer and employee experiences
- define how KCS differs from current knowledge management practices and identify the opportunities it creates
- establish the preliminary approach and next steps towards a centralised knowledge system that supports both employees and customers across business and service channels

Use this goal to improve how information foundation work is explained and adopted.

For each major stakeholder group, consider:

| Stakeholder group | What they need from this goal |
|---|---|
| CX team | Shared understanding of how IA, account quality and knowledge connect |
| Customer Data Officer | Clear account quality priorities, standards and roadmap logic |
| Information Officer | Clear service information and knowledge quality expectations |
| Contact Centre | More consistent service information, routing logic and customer context |
| Digital / web teams | Clearer customer task language, IA patterns and source-of-truth logic |
| CRM / Salesforce teams | Better account management rules, process clarity and data-quality priorities |
| Service owners | Clearer ownership of service information, account processes and knowledge content |
| Governance partners | Practical translation of privacy, records, security and data governance into day-to-day service rules |
| Leaders | Simple narrative showing why information foundations matter for connected customer experiences |

## Quarterly review rhythm

### Q1

Focus:

- clarify the scope of IA, account management and KCS work
- identify priority pain points and discovery playback needs
- align with Channel Strategy Year 2 and Intelligent Front Door work
- identify stakeholder ownership and governance dependencies

Evidence to capture:

- baseline artefact map
- priority account management pain points
- early IA framework outputs
- KCS discovery framing
- stakeholder alignment needs

### Q2

Focus:

- refine IA framework and Intelligent Front Door logic
- progress account management standardisation
- develop roadmap logic
- shape KCS preliminary approach

Evidence to capture:

- IA framework updates
- Intelligent Front Door outputs
- account management process standards
- roadmap draft
- KCS comparison or opportunity notes

### Q3

Focus:

- test adoption of information foundation artefacts
- refine roadmap and standards based on stakeholder feedback

- examples of reuse
- stakeholder feedback
- standardisation evidence
- clearer governance or ownership logic

### Q4

Focus:

- consolidate progress and evidence
- identify what became useful and reusable

- recommend next-year improvements

Evidence to capture:

- final artefact summary
- decisions influenced
- roadmap progress
- adoption evidence
- lessons learned
- career proof points
