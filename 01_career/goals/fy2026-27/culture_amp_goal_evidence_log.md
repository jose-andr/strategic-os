# FY2026/27 Culture Amp Goal Evidence Log

## Status

Draft

## Source system

Culture Amp

## Strategic OS role

Culture Amp remains the formal system of record for goal status and official performance documentation.

Strategic OS is used to capture safe evidence, interpretation, learning and career proof points throughout the year.

This file should be updated regularly so that quarterly Culture Amp updates and end-of-year performance reflections are easier to prepare.

## Goals covered

1. Business Goal 1 — Create a cohesive set of CX Strategy tools
2. Business Goal 2 — Build strong customer and business information foundations to enable great customer experiences
3. Business Goal 3 — To be confirmed
4. Development Goal — Develop AI-agentic strategic design practices to augment complex CX work and build team capability
5. Context Goal — Live our ICARE values
6. Context Goal — Make our day-to-day work better

## How to use this file

Use this file to record evidence when work creates value against one or more Culture Amp goals.

Capture evidence when:

- a decision is clarified
- a stakeholder group becomes more aligned
- a reusable artefact, prompt, template or framework is created
- a team member is enabled to adopt or support the work
- a customer or employee experience issue becomes clearer
- a blind spot is identified
- an unproductive rabbit hole is avoided
- a Culture Amp action progresses
- a useful lesson is learned
- a strong career proof point emerges

Do not wait until the end of the quarter. Capture short notes while the context is fresh.

## Evidence quality standard

Good evidence should be:

- specific
- safe to store
- linked to a goal
- clear about the outcome
- clear about the role I played
- clear about the decision, alignment or capability improved
- caveated where needed
- reusable for quarterly or EOFY reflection

Avoid evidence that is:

- too vague
- only a task update
- based on raw organisational data
- dependent on sensitive customer or operational detail
- unclear about impact
- unclear about what I contributed

## Privacy and source-of-truth note

Do not store:

- raw organisational data
- customer records
- dashboard extracts
- sensitive operational details
- credentials
- access tokens
- unredacted screenshots
- identifiable case examples
- controlled documents that belong in another system of record

Store only:

- summaries
- caveats
- source rules
- metric definitions
- interpretation
- decision logic
- reusable prompts
- stakeholder patterns
- lessons learned
- career evidence

Culture Amp remains the formal source of truth for goal status.

Power BI, Service Account dashboards, CRM, Databricks, SharePoint, Jira, Confluence, Teams and other organisational platforms remain the systems of record for operational information and original working material.
