## Strategic OS role

Strategic OS does not replace Culture Amp.

Culture Amp remains the formal system of record for goals, progress status, ownership, due dates and official performance documentation.

Strategic OS is used to interpret these goals strategically and turn them into:

- weekly and monthly steering prompts;
- quarterly evidence capture;
- decision and stakeholder-alignment logic;
- career proof points;
- reusable strategic design patterns;
- team enablement opportunities;
- end-of-year performance narrative.

- builds reusable CX strategy capability;

- improves stakeholder alignment;

- creates visible career evidence;

This portfolio supports the Strategic OS desired reputation:

## Organisation-wide context goals

### Context goal 1 — Live our ICARE values

Culture Amp asks employees to identify how they can demonstrate one or more ICARE values in their role.

The values shown are:

- Integrity — being honest and reliable
- Courage — stepping out of your comfort zone
- Accountability — taking responsibility for decisions
- Respect — recognising contributions and ideas
- Excellence — aiming to achieve the highest standard

### How this informs Strategic OS

Use ICARE as a behavioural lens when reviewing goal progress.

Strategic OS reflection prompts:

- Integrity: Have I been clear about evidence, assumptions, caveats and limits?
- Courage: Have I stepped into ambiguity or difficult alignment work rather than avoiding it?
- Accountability: Have I made ownership, decisions and next actions visible?
- Respect: Have I recognised the contributions, constraints and knowledge of others?
- Excellence: Have I improved the quality, clarity or usefulness of the work?

### Context goal 2 — Make our day-to-day work better

Culture Amp asks what steps I will take to improve the way we work together as an organisation and with customers and community.

### How this informs Strategic OS

Use this as an operating-improvement lens across the portfolio.

Strategic OS reflection prompts:

- Did this work make day-to-day decisions clearer?
- Did it reduce rework, confusion or duplicated effort?
- Did it improve how teams work together?
- Did it improve the customer or employee experience?
- Did it create a reusable artefact, standard, prompt, dashboard interpretation or working pattern?

## Goal 1 — Create a cohesive set of CX Strategy tools

### Culture Amp goal title

Create a cohesive set of CX Strategy tools

### Goal type

Individual business goal

### Due date

30 June 2027

### Culture Amp intent

Lead the next phase of the organisation’s customer-centric transformation by bringing together CX Segments and Modes, Channel Strategy and the CX Roadmap into a cohesive set of tools and practices.

The goal is to enable the Customer Experience team and the broader organisation to consistently put customers at the centre of decision-making.

### Key result areas

1. Deliver and embed the Organisational Customer Strategy
2. Define the point of view on a connected channel experience
3. Maintain and evolve the organisational CX Roadmap

### Strategic OS interpretation

This goal is about turning customer strategy from a set of separate artefacts into a practical organisational decision-support system.

The strategic opportunity is to make CX strategy tools easier to understand, easier to apply and more useful for business decision-making.

### Strategic contribution

Use this goal to demonstrate capability in:

- strategic synthesis;
- customer-centred decision-making;
- organisational storytelling;
- roadmap facilitation;
- stakeholder alignment;
- reusable tool creation;
- strategy-to-delivery translation.

### Steering questions

Use these during monthly review:

1. Which CX strategy tool is most important this month?
2. What decision or behaviour should this tool help improve?
3. Who needs to understand or adopt it?
4. Is the tool clear enough for others to use without me explaining it every time?
5. What evidence shows the tool is influencing business thinking or prioritisation?
6. What should be simplified, socialised or reused?

### Evidence to capture

- CX Strategy tools created or refined
- Customer strategy artefacts
- Connected channel principles
- CX Roadmap updates
- Roadmap session outputs
- Stakeholder feedback
- Decisions influenced
- Examples of customer-centred language being adopted
- Reusable templates or patterns

### Career proof point

This goal should show that I can help the organisation move from fragmented CX artefacts to a coherent strategy toolkit that supports better customer-centred decisions.

## Goal 2 — Build strong customer and business information foundations to enable great customer experiences

### Culture Amp goal title

Build strong customer and business information foundations to enable great customer experiences

### Goal type

Individual business goal

### Due date

30 June 2027

### Culture Amp intent

Strengthen data, information and knowledge foundations so customers can access consistent, accurate and connected information across channels.

This goal recognises that strong information foundations reduce duplication, improve service continuity and create better customer and employee experiences.

### Key result areas

1. Strengthen Information Architecture
2. Improve Customer Account Management
3. Establish a Knowledge-Centred Service approach

### Strategic OS interpretation

This goal is about making connected customer experiences possible by improving the quality, structure and governance of the information that sits underneath them.

The strategic opportunity is to connect information architecture, account management and knowledge management into a coherent service foundation rather than treating them as separate improvement activities.

### Strategic contribution

Use this goal to demonstrate capability in:

- systems thinking;
- service information architecture;
- customer account quality;
- knowledge management discovery;
- governance translation;
- cross-functional alignment;
- customer and employee experience improvement;
- service foundation design.

### Steering questions

Use these during monthly review:

1. Which information foundation is most important this month: IA, account management or knowledge?
2. What customer or employee problem does it help solve?
3. What is the decision or alignment needed?
4. What needs to become a repeatable standard, pattern or roadmap item?
5. Where are the dependencies with Channel Strategy Year 2, CRM, Service Account or knowledge work?
6. What should be captured as reusable Strategic OS learning?

### Evidence to capture

- IA framework development
- Discovery outputs
- Account management pain points
- Customer Account Management roadmap
- Standardised business process approaches
- KCS discovery findings
- Knowledge management comparison notes
- Cross-channel information patterns
- Stakeholder alignment decisions
- Risks, assumptions and caveats

### Career proof point

This goal should show that I can translate complex information, data and knowledge challenges into practical service foundations that improve customer continuity and organisational readiness.

## Goal 4 — Development goal

### Culture Amp goal title

Develop AI-agentic strategic design practices to augment complex CX work and build team capability

### Goal type

Individual development goal

### Due date

30 June 2027

### Culture Amp intent

Develop and test AI-agentic ways of working that strengthen my practice as a strategic design and CX systems practitioner while supporting team capability growth.

The goal focuses on using AI responsibly to augment complex CX work by improving problem framing, synthesis, option testing, blind-spot identification, avoiding unproductive rabbit holes, separating evidence from assumptions, creating reusable artefacts and communicating decision logic.

### Actions

1. Create and test at least three repeatable AI-assisted working patterns for CX strategy and design work.
2. Apply the AI-assisted approach to at least three live pieces of work.
3. Enable Rebecca to use the Service Account dashboard to provide quarterly updates and EOFY insights.
4. Contribute at least one practical AI-enabled learning or prototyping activity linked to Team Goal 6.
5. Capture lessons learned into a simple reusable guide or reflection.

### Strategic OS interpretation

This goal is the capability engine for the rest of the portfolio.

It should help me improve how I:

- frame complex CX problems;
- identify decisions and alignment needs;
- synthesise evidence;
- spot blind spots;
- avoid rabbit holes;
- communicate the logic of the work;
- bring people along the journey;
- enable others to adopt and support the work;
- capture reusable strategic knowledge.

### Strategic contribution

Use this goal to demonstrate capability in:

- responsible AI-enabled work;
- strategic design practice development;
- stakeholder journey design;
- repeatable method creation;
- team enablement;
- dashboard interpretation support;
- reusable prompt and artefact design;
- career-long capability development.

### Steering questions

Use these during monthly review:

1. What did I use AI to augment this month?
2. Did it improve decision clarity, stakeholder alignment or artefact quality?
3. Did it reveal a blind spot I may have missed?
4. Did it help me avoid an unproductive rabbit hole?
5. Did it help me bring someone along the journey?
6. Did any output become repeatable?
7. Did any team member adopt, support or use the work?
8. What evidence should be captured for Culture Amp or career development?

### Evidence to capture

- AI-assisted working patterns
- Reusable prompts
- Live work examples
- Service Account dashboard enablement artefacts
- Rebecca enablement progress
- Team Goal 6 contribution
- Lessons learned
- Examples of clearer communication
- Examples of decisions or stakeholder alignment improved
- Reflections on where human judgement was essential

### Career proof point

This goal should show that I can use AI responsibly to augment strategic CX work while creating repeatable, communicable and team-adoptable ways of working.
