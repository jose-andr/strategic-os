## Status

Draft

## Culture Amp title

Develop AI-agentic strategic design practices to augment complex CX work and build team capability

## Due date

30 June 2027

## Strategic OS role

Culture Amp remains the formal system of record for the submitted goal, progress status and performance documentation.

Strategic OS is used to make the goal operational throughout the year by capturing:

- reusable AI-assisted working patterns
- evidence of clearer decision logic
- examples of stakeholder alignment
- team enablement outcomes
- lessons learned
- career proof points
- safe reflections on responsible AI use

## Goal statement

This FY, I will develop and test AI-agentic ways of working that strengthen my practice as a strategic design and CX systems practitioner, while supporting our team goal to grow our capability as a Strategy and Design Team through learning, experimentation and practical application.

My work often involves complex CX challenges across customer strategy, channel experience, CRM adoption, information architecture, knowledge management, customer data, governance, stakeholder alignment and delivery planning. These areas are connected, but are often managed through separate tools, artefacts and conversations.

I want to use AI responsibly to augment this work — not by replacing judgement, but by improving how I frame problems, synthesise information, test options, identify blind spots, avoid unproductive rabbit holes, separate evidence from assumptions, create reusable artefacts and communicate decision logic.

A key development focus is making this practice repeatable, communicable and useful to others. I want to improve how I bring people along the journey by creating clearer artefacts, explaining recommendations more effectively and enabling team members to adopt and support the work.

By the end of the FY, I aim to demonstrate a practical, repeatable approach for AI-augmented strategic design work that improves my effectiveness and supports team capability.

## Manager feedback to amplify

This goal should explicitly strengthen my ability to:

1. Repeat the work  
   Develop methods and artefacts that can be reused across more than one project or problem.

2. Communicate the work  
   Make the logic, evidence, assumptions, decisions and recommendations easier for others to understand.

3. Enable others to adopt and support the work  
   Help team members use the approach, dashboard, artefact or practice without depending on me each time.

## Development shift

    AI-augmented personal productivity
            ↓
    Repeatable, communicable and team-enabled strategic design practice

## Why this matters

This goal supports the Strategic OS desired reputation:

> He helps us make better strategic decisions.

The goal is not only about using AI to work faster. It is about using AI responsibly to strengthen judgement, improve decision clarity, bring people along the journey and create reusable team capability.

## Priority test case — Service Account reporting enablement

### Intent

Support Rebecca, as Customer Data Officer, to use the Service Account dashboard as a repeatable reporting and insight tool.

### Desired shift

    Rebecca relies on ad hoc support to interpret Service Account performance
            ↓
    Rebecca understands what the dashboard is showing
            ↓
    Rebecca can use the dashboard to provide quarterly updates
            ↓
    Rebecca can identify key trends, caveats and insights
            ↓
    Rebecca can provide an EOFY view of how the Service Account performed

### Enablement artefacts to create

- plain-English dashboard explanation
- metric interpretation guide
- quarterly update structure
- caveat checklist
- prompt for turning observations into insight statements
- review process before updates are shared more broadly

### Evidence to capture

- what Rebecca needed to understand
- what artefact or explanation helped
- what questions or blind spots emerged
- what could be repeated next quarter
- whether Rebecca became more confident and independent
- what this demonstrates about team enablement

## Quarterly steering questions

Use these during quarterly review:

1. What did I use AI to augment this quarter?
2. Did it improve decision clarity, stakeholder alignment or artefact quality?
3. Did it reveal a blind spot I may have missed?
4. Did it help me avoid an unproductive rabbit hole?
5. Did it help me bring someone along the journey?
6. Did any output become repeatable?
7. Did any team member adopt, support or use the work?
8. What evidence should be captured for Culture Amp or career development?
9. What should I stop, refine, socialise or scale next quarter?
10. What should be discussed with my manager at the next check-in?

## Quarterly review rhythm

### Q1

Focus:

- establish baseline practice
- identify candidate workflows
- confirm Service Account reporting enablement need
- test first AI-assisted patterns

Evidence to capture:

- first working patterns
- early blind spots
- examples of AI improving framing or communication
- initial Rebecca enablement plan

### Q2

Focus:

- apply patterns to live work
- support first or second quarterly Service Account update
- contribute to Team Goal 6 planning or activity
- refine communication and stakeholder-alignment patterns

Evidence to capture:

- live work examples
- dashboard interpretation support
- team feedback
- reusable artefacts

### Q3

Focus:

- improve repeatability
- reduce reliance on ad hoc support
- strengthen team adoption
- prepare EOFY evidence narrative

Evidence to capture:

- examples of team member independence
- improved prompts or templates
- evidence of clearer communication
- examples of decisions shaped or supported

### Q4

Focus:

- complete EOFY Service Account reporting support
- capture final lessons learned
- convert evidence into performance and career narrative
- identify what should continue next FY

Evidence to capture:

- Rebecca EOFY reporting capability
- final reusable guide or reflection
- Culture Amp update
- career proof points
- next-year development opportunities

- caveats

- interpretation
- reusable prompts
- decision logic
- stakeholder patterns
- lessons learned
- career evidence
