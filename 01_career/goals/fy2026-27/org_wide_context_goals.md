## Strategic OS role

Culture Amp remains the formal system of record for organisation-wide goals.

Strategic OS is used to translate these context goals into practical reflection prompts, behavioural evidence and operating principles that can guide how I deliver my business and development goals throughout FY2026/27.

These goals should not be treated as separate tasks. They should be used as lenses for how the work is done.

## Context goals

1. Live our ICARE values
2. Make our day-to-day work better

The organisation-wide goals should shape how I deliver:

- Business Goal 1 — Create a cohesive set of CX Strategy tools
- Business Goal 2 — Build strong customer and business information foundations to enable great customer experiences
- Business Goal 3 — To be confirmed
- Development Goal — Develop AI-agentic strategic design practices to augment complex CX work and build team capability

## Context goal 1 — Live our ICARE values

### Culture Amp title

Live our ICARE values

### Strategic OS interpretation

Use the ICARE values as a behavioural lens for how I show up in complex CX, strategy, design, stakeholder and AI-enabled work.

This is especially relevant where the work involves ambiguity, competing priorities, uncertain evidence, stakeholder tension or decisions that require careful judgement.

## ICARE reflection lens

### Integrity

Use Integrity to check whether the work is honest, evidence-aware and clear about its limits.

Reflection prompts:

- Have I clearly separated evidence, assumptions, interpretation and recommendation?
- Have I been honest about uncertainty, caveats or data limitations?
- Have I avoided overstating what the evidence supports?
- Have I protected privacy and avoided storing raw organisational or customer data in Strategic OS?
- Have I communicated the real trade-offs rather than simplifying them too far?

Evidence to capture:

- caveated recommendations
- clear decision briefs
- examples of evidence-safe communication
- privacy-safe summaries
- examples where I corrected or clarified a misleading interpretation

### Courage

Use Courage to check whether I am stepping into complexity rather than avoiding it.

Reflection prompts:

- Have I stepped into an ambiguous or uncomfortable strategic problem?
- Have I challenged a weak assumption or unclear decision?
- Have I helped name a risk, dependency or blind spot early?
- Have I tried a new AI-agentic practice responsibly?
- Have I helped the team experiment with new ways of working?

Evidence to capture:

- examples of difficult framing work
- risk or blind-spot identification
- responsible experimentation
- stakeholder conversations that required courage
- Team Goal 6 learning or prototyping contributions

### Accountability

Use Accountability to check whether decisions, actions and ownership are visible.

Reflection prompts:

- Have I made the decision or next action clear?
- Have I clarified who needs to own, approve, support or adopt the work?
- Have I followed through on evidence capture or stakeholder alignment?
- Have I made progress visible through the right system of record?
- Have I avoided leaving important logic only in my head?

Evidence to capture:

- decision logs
- clear next actions
- stakeholder alignment notes
- progress updates
- examples of translating strategy into delivery activity

### Respect

Use Respect to check whether I am bringing people along the journey.

Reflection prompts:

- Have I recognised the expertise, constraints and contributions of others?
- Have I adapted the message for the audience?
- Have I made complex work easier for others to engage with?
- Have I created space for team members to adopt, question or improve the work?
- Have I supported team enablement rather than creating dependency on me?

Evidence to capture:

- stakeholder feedback
- team enablement examples
- Rebecca Service Account reporting enablement
- clearer artefacts that others can use
- examples of co-design, playback or collaborative refinement

### Excellence

Use Excellence to check whether the work improves quality, usefulness and strategic value.

Reflection prompts:

- Has the work improved decision clarity?
- Has it improved stakeholder alignment?
- Has it created a reusable artefact, pattern, prompt or tool?
- Has it improved customer or employee experience?
- Has it reduced confusion, rework or duplication?
- Has it strengthened my career positioning as a strategic CX and design practitioner?

Evidence to capture:

- reusable tools
- improved frameworks
- clearer roadmap outputs
- better dashboard interpretation
- stronger stakeholder narratives
- end-of-year career proof points

## Context goal 2 — Make our day-to-day work better

### Culture Amp title

Make our day-to-day work better

### Strategic OS interpretation

Use this goal as an operating-improvement lens across my FY goal portfolio.

The focus is not only on delivering outputs. The focus is also on improving how work gets done: reducing friction, clarifying decisions, making artefacts easier to use, improving handover, supporting repeatability and enabling better collaboration.

## Day-to-day improvement lens

Use this goal to test whether work is improving the operating environment around me.

Reflection prompts:

- Did this work make a decision easier?
- Did it reduce rework, duplication or confusion?
- Did it make stakeholder expectations clearer?
- Did it make an artefact easier for others to use?
- Did it improve how strategy connects to delivery?
- Did it help a team member work more independently?
- Did it create a reusable working pattern?
- Did it improve the customer or employee experience?
- Did it reduce reliance on informal explanation or personal memory?

## Practical applications

### CX Strategy tools

Use the day-to-day improvement lens to check whether CX Strategy tools are genuinely usable.

Questions:

- Can stakeholders understand the tool without a long explanation?
- Does the tool help people make better customer-centred decisions?
- Does it reduce confusion between CX Segments, Modes, Channel Strategy and Roadmap work?
- Does it help the business prioritise or plan with the customer in mind?

### Information foundations

Use the day-to-day improvement lens to check whether IA, account management and knowledge work are improving practical service foundations.

Questions:

- Does this reduce ambiguity around service information?
- Does this clarify account management pain points or ownership?
- Does this help staff find, trust or use information more consistently?
- Does this reduce avoidable handoffs, rework or duplicated effort?

### AI-agentic development goal

Use the day-to-day improvement lens to check whether AI is improving real work, not just creating more output.

Questions:

- Did AI help identify a blind spot?
- Did AI help avoid an unproductive rabbit hole?
- Did AI help communicate the work more clearly?
- Did AI help create something reusable?
- Did AI help someone else adopt or support the work?

### Team enablement

Use the day-to-day improvement lens to check whether the work is becoming adoptable by others.

Questions:

- Can another team member repeat the process?
- Can Rebecca use the Service Account dashboard to provide quarterly updates?
- Can the team reuse the prompt, template, artefact or workflow?
- Has the work reduced dependency on me as the only interpreter?

## End-of-year narrative

By the end of FY2026/27, these organisation-wide goals should help show not only what I delivered, but how I delivered it.

The narrative should show that I:

- acted with integrity by using evidence carefully and making caveats visible
- showed courage by stepping into ambiguity and testing new AI-enabled practices
- demonstrated accountability by making decisions, ownership and next actions clearer
- showed respect by bringing people along the journey and enabling team members
- pursued excellence by creating reusable strategic capability and improving decision quality
- made day-to-day work better by reducing confusion, improving artefacts and strengthening collaboration

## Privacy and source-of-truth note

Culture Amp remains the formal source of truth for organisation-wide goal status.

Strategic OS should only store safe summaries, reflection prompts, behavioural evidence, decision logic, stakeholder patterns, lessons learned and career proof points.

Do not store raw organisational data, customer records, sensitive operational extracts, credentials or unredacted screenshots in Strategic OS.
