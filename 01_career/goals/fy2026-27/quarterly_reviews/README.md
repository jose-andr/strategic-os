# FY2026/27 Culture Amp Quarterly Reviews

## Status

Draft

## Purpose

This folder holds quarterly Strategic OS review files for the FY2026/27 Culture Amp goal portfolio.

Use these files to capture safe summaries of progress, evidence, stakeholder alignment, team enablement, AI-agentic practice development and career proof points.

Culture Amp remains the formal system of record for submitted goals, progress status and official performance documentation.

Strategic OS is used to prepare, interpret and strengthen quarterly updates.

## Review files

| File | Period covered | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `q1_goal_review.md` | July to September 2026 | Capture early progress, setup, evidence opportunities and Q2 steering actions. |
| `q2_goal_review.md` | October to December 2026 | Capture mid-cycle progress, early impact, reusable artefacts and Q3 steering actions. |
| `q3_goal_review.md` | January to March 2027 | Capture adoption, team enablement, reusable capability and Q4 completion priorities. |
| `q4_eofy_goal_review.md` | April to June 2027 | Consolidate final evidence, EOFY Culture Amp update and career narrative. |

## Quarterly review rhythm

At the end of each quarter:

1. Open the relevant quarterly review file.
2. Update the goal status snapshot.
3. Capture evidence against each business, development and context goal.
4. Identify decisions clarified, stakeholders aligned and artefacts made reusable.
5. Record team enablement examples.
6. Draft the Culture Amp quarterly update.
7. Transfer strong proof points into `culture_amp_goal_evidence_log.md`.
8. Set next-quarter steering actions.

## Evidence standard

Evidence should be:

- specific
- safe to store
- linked to one or more Culture Amp goals
- clear about the outcome
- clear about my contribution
- clear about the decision, alignment or capability improved
- caveated where needed
- useful for quarterly updates or EOFY reflection

## Privacy and source-of-truth note

Do not store raw organisational data, customer records, dashboard extracts, credentials, sensitive operational details, identifiable case examples or unredacted screenshots in these review files.

Store only:

- safe summaries
- caveats
- source rules
- metric definitions
- interpretation
- decision logic
- reusable prompts
- stakeholder patterns
- lessons learned
- career evidence
