## Source-of-truth rule

Culture Amp remains the formal system of record for goal status and official performance documentation.

Strategic OS is used to capture safe summaries, interpretation, decision logic, reusable patterns, stakeholder learning and career evidence.

Do not store raw organisational data, customer records, dashboard extracts, sensitive operational details, credentials or unredacted screenshots in this review.

## Q1 review intent

Use this review to capture early progress, clarify what needs attention and identify evidence that can support Culture Amp updates and career development conversations.

Q1 should focus on:

- establishing the goal portfolio operating rhythm
- confirming the business goal structure
- identifying early evidence opportunities
- setting up the AI-agentic development goal
- clarifying Service Account reporting enablement needs
- identifying what needs to be steered in Q2
