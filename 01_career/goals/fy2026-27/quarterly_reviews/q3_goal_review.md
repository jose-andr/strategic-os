## Source-of-truth rule

Culture Amp remains the formal system of record for goal status and official performance documentation.

Strategic OS is used to capture safe summaries, interpretation, decision logic, reusable patterns, stakeholder learning and career evidence.

Do not store raw organisational data, customer records, dashboard extracts, sensitive operational details, credentials or unredacted screenshots in this review.

## Q3 review intent

Use this review to assess whether the goal portfolio is translating into visible progress, reusable capability and team enablement.

Q3 should focus on:

- strengthening adoption of CX Strategy tools
- testing whether information foundation artefacts are being used or understood
- reducing reliance on ad hoc support
- capturing evidence of team member independence
- preparing EOFY evidence early
- identifying what needs to be completed, refined or deprioritised in Q4
