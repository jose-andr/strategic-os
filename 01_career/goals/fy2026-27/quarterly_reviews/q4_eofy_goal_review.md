## Source-of-truth rule

Culture Amp remains the formal system of record for goal status and official performance documentation.

Strategic OS is used to capture safe summaries, interpretation, decision logic, reusable patterns, stakeholder learning and career evidence.

Do not store raw organisational data, customer records, dashboard extracts, sensitive operational details, credentials or unredacted screenshots in this review.

## Q4 / EOFY review intent

Use this review to consolidate final progress across the FY2026/27 Culture Amp goal portfolio and prepare the end-of-year performance and career narrative.

Q4 should focus on:

- finalising evidence against each goal
- identifying what was delivered, improved or influenced
- showing how decisions became clearer
- showing how stakeholders were brought along
- capturing reusable artefacts, tools, prompts or patterns
- demonstrating team enablement
- reflecting on AI-agentic strategic design practice
- preparing next-year development signals
