# Opportunity Summary

## What Is The Opportunity?

There is an opportunity to establish clearer leadership and ownership for an emerging CX capability spanning:

- customer account quality;
- service information quality;
- customer information architecture;
- continuous improvement;
- practical governance; and
- connected-interactions readiness.

The proposed organisational mechanism is to evolve the existing Project Officer Customer Focus role into a Class/Band 6 Customer Accounts and Information Services Lead role.

The strategic opportunity is broader than the position change.

It is the opportunity to become responsible for shaping and leading an integrated service capability that connects customer, organisational, information and technology outcomes.

## Why Now?

The opportunity has emerged because several current CX priorities increasingly depend on the same underlying foundations.

These include:

- Channel Strategy Y2;
- customer account management;
- information architecture;
- knowledge-centred service;
- CRM adoption;
- digital customer experience;
- Service Account improvements; and
- connected customer interactions.

Without clearer ownership, related work risks continuing as separate tasks, projects and improvement activities.

## What Triggered It?

The opportunity has been triggered by the convergence of:

- existing customer account quality work;
- existing service information responsibilities;
- Channel Strategy Y2 information architecture requirements;
- FY2026/27 CX priorities;
- the existence of Information Officer and Customer Data Officer capability;
- the need for stronger coordination across CRM, knowledge, digital and service-delivery work.

## Who Is Involved?

Relevant groups include:

- Customer Focus and Strategy leadership;
- CX branch leadership;
- Information Officer;
- Customer Data Officer;
- CRM / Salesforce partners;
- Digital / channel partners;
- knowledge and service information partners;
- data and information governance partners;
- service owners;
- workforce / classification advisers.

Individual positions are not assumed.

# Strategic Fit

## Connection To Strategic Direction

The opportunity strongly aligns with current CX direction.

It connects several active priorities that would otherwise risk being treated separately:

- connected customer interactions;
- customer account management;
- information architecture;
- knowledge-centred service;
- CRM improvement;
- digital experience;
- customer information quality.

The strongest strategic value is therefore not the role title.

It is the ability to create a coherent operating capability across related work.

## Connection To The North Star

Strategic OS North Star:

> Increase strategic opportunities by helping leaders make better strategic decisions.

This opportunity supports that North Star in two ways.

First, the work itself requires leaders to make clearer decisions about:

- service ownership;
- information quality;
- customer account management;
- channel interaction design;
- CRM priorities;
- governance boundaries.

Second, successfully shaping the capability could create future strategic opportunities by demonstrating an ability to connect fragmented organisational problems into a coherent operating model.

- clearer service pathways;

- better integration between customer data and service information;

High.

- people;

- service architecture;

It therefore provides a strong opportunity to demonstrate systems thinking rather than isolated project delivery.

# Recommendation

Recommended path:

**Socialise first, then shape further toward pursuit.**

## Rationale

The opportunity has high strategic, organisational and career value.

However, immediate formal pursuit would be premature because:

- stakeholder support has not been validated;
- role boundaries remain open;
- classification has not been assessed;
- decision rights remain unclear.

The strongest path is therefore:

> validate the capability need → shape the operating model → confirm organisational pathway → pursue if supported.

This preserves strategic credibility and prevents the opportunity from becoming centred on personal role advancement.

## Confidence

**High confidence**

Reason:

The strategic alignment is strong and supported by multiple current CX priorities.

The uncertainty lies primarily in the organisational mechanism, not the existence of the underlying opportunity.
