# Culture Amp Goal Steering Prompt Pack

## Status

Draft

## Purpose

Use this prompt pack to support quarterly Culture Amp goal reviews, evidence capture and career narrative building.

The prompts are designed to help turn goal activity into:

- clearer decision logic
- stronger evidence summaries
- stakeholder-alignment insights
- team-enablement proof points
- safer Culture Amp updates
- end-of-year career narrative

## Source-of-truth rule

Culture Amp remains the formal source of truth for submitted goals, progress status and official performance documentation.

Strategic OS is used to capture safe summaries, interpretation, reusable patterns, decision logic, stakeholder learning and career evidence.

Do not paste raw organisational data, customer records, dashboard extracts, sensitive operational details, credentials or unredacted screenshots into AI tools.

Use safe summaries only.

## Prompt 1 — Quarterly goal review

Use this prompt at the end of each quarter.

```text
Act as my Strategic OS career and goal-steering partner.

I am reviewing my FY2026/27 Culture Amp goals for the quarter.

Help me turn the notes below into a clear quarterly review that shows:
- progress against each goal
- evidence of impact
- decisions clarified
- stakeholders brought along
- reusable artefacts or patterns created
- team enablement outcomes
- AI-agentic practice development
- risks, blockers or caveats
- next quarter priorities

Use safe, plain-English language suitable for Culture Amp and manager discussion.

Do not overclaim. Separate evidence, assumption, interpretation and next action.

My notes:
[PASTE SAFE NOTES]
```

## Prompt 3 — Culture Amp progress update

Use this prompt to draft a concise update for Culture Amp.

```text
Act as my Culture Amp update editor.

Using the evidence below, draft a concise quarterly progress update.

The update should:
- sound professional and manager-ready
- focus on outcomes, not only activity
- mention evidence of progress
- include stakeholder or team enablement where relevant
- include AI-agentic practice development where relevant
- be honest about risks or blockers
- avoid overclaiming
- avoid sensitive operational detail

Structure:
Progress summary:
Key evidence:
Stakeholder or team enablement:
Risks or blockers:
Next quarter focus:

Evidence:
[PASTE SAFE EVIDENCE]
```

## Prompt 4 — Decision clarity check

Use this prompt before turning work into an artefact, slide or update.

```text
Act as a decision-clarity reviewer.

Review the notes below and help me clarify:
- what decision this work supports
- who needs to decide, support or adopt the work
- what evidence is available
- what assumptions need to be visible
- what caveats should be stated
- what options or trade-offs should be compared
- what recommendation is emerging
- what next action should be proposed

Also identify any blind spots or rabbit holes I should avoid.

Notes:
[PASTE SAFE NOTES]
```

## Prompt 5 — Bring people along the journey

Use this prompt when preparing stakeholder communication.

```text
Act as a stakeholder-alignment coach.

I need to bring people along the journey for the work described below.

Help me create a communication approach that explains:
- why this work matters
- what problem we are solving
- what has been learned so far
- what decision or alignment is needed
- what trade-offs or caveats should be visible
- what stakeholders may be concerned about
- how to explain the work simply
- what artefact or conversation would help
- what the next step should be

Make the response practical, plain-English and suitable for a workplace CX/strategy context.

Work summary:
[PASTE SAFE SUMMARY]
```

## Prompt 6 — Team enablement check

Use this prompt when testing whether the work is adoptable by others.

```text
Act as a team-enablement reviewer.

Review the work described below and assess whether it is repeatable, communicable and adoptable by another team member.

Assess:
- what the team member needs to understand
- what is currently too implicit
- what explanation, guide, prompt, dashboard view or artefact would help
- what support is needed for first use
- what evidence would show growing independence
- what should be simplified
- what should be captured as reusable capability

Use the specific example of enabling a team member to use a dashboard, artefact or working pattern independently where relevant.

Work summary:
[PASTE SAFE SUMMARY]
```

## Prompt 7 — Service Account dashboard enablement

Use this prompt for the Rebecca Service Account reporting test case.

```text
Act as a dashboard interpretation and team-enablement coach.

I am supporting Rebecca, the Customer Data Officer, to use the Service Account dashboard for quarterly updates and EOFY insights.

Help me create a simple enablement plan that covers:
- what Rebecca needs to understand
- the key dashboard views or metrics to explain
- how to turn observations into plain-English insights
- what caveats or data-quality limits should be checked
- what a quarterly update structure could look like
- what questions Rebecca should ask when reviewing the dashboard
- what evidence would show she is becoming more confident and independent
- what support I should provide without creating dependency

Use safe, general language. Do not require raw dashboard data.

Current situation:
[PASTE SAFE SUMMARY]
```

## Prompt 8 — Blind spot and rabbit hole review

Use this prompt before investing significant time in a piece of work.

```text
Act as a strategic blind-spot and rabbit-hole reviewer.

Review the work described below and identify:
- possible blind spots
- assumptions I may be making
- stakeholder perspectives I may be missing
- evidence gaps
- areas where I may be overcomplicating the work
- rabbit holes to avoid
- what is good enough for the next decision
- the smallest useful next action

Keep the advice practical and decision-focused.

Work summary:
[PASTE SAFE SUMMARY]
```

## Prompt 9 — End-of-year career narrative

Use this prompt near EOFY.

```text
Act as my Strategic OS career narrative partner.

Using the evidence below, help me draft an end-of-year performance and career narrative.

The narrative should show:
- what changed because of my work
- how I improved decision clarity
- how I strengthened stakeholder alignment
- how I created reusable tools, prompts, frameworks or patterns
- how I enabled team members
- how I used AI responsibly
- where human judgement was essential
- how I demonstrated ICARE values
- how I made day-to-day work better
- how the evidence supports the reputation: “He helps us make better strategic decisions.”

Avoid overclaiming. Use plain-English, workplace-appropriate language.

Evidence:
[PASTE SAFE EVIDENCE]
```
