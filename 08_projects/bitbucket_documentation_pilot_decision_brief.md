# Bitbucket Documentation Pilot — Decision Brief

## Status

Draft for stakeholder discussion

## Decision required

Should the team run a contained pilot using Bitbucket to test a reusable, governed and machine-readable documentation approach for selected internal project artefacts and customer-facing knowledge assets?

## Recommendation

Approve a small pilot using existing City of Melbourne Bitbucket capability.

The pilot should test a limited set of non-sensitive assets, a simple cross-team governance workflow and reuse across two or three publishing channels.

The pilot should produce evidence before any decision to scale.

## Opportunity

We have an opportunity to create a more consistent way to maintain and reuse project knowledge, design artefacts and customer information across the team and branch.

The approach could support two connected purposes:

1. improving how teams create, maintain and reuse internal project knowledge; and
2. providing governed source material for consistent customer-facing information across channels.

The opportunity extends beyond conventional project documentation.

It includes reusable strategic, service-design and customer-information assets such as:

- service-design patterns and design elements;
- customer journey and connected-interaction patterns;
- customer information architecture;
- customer-friendly service names and descriptions;
- mappings between internal terminology, system labels and customer language;
- customer segmentation profiles and supporting research;
- channel, routing and response-mode principles;
- service definitions and decision records;
- publishing and governance rules;
- reusable prompts and agent instructions; and
- structured knowledge suitable for controlled machine interpretation.

## Why this matters now

Several emerging bodies of work could benefit from a shared documentation model.

Channel Strategy Y2 is expected to produce reusable assets including:

- connected-interaction patterns;
- customer intent structures;
- customer information architecture;
- routing and channel principles;
- journey status patterns; and
- journey measurement approaches.

Customer segmentation research is also ready to move from a research output into practical use.

Several AI-agent experiments across City of Melbourne may benefit from clearer, governed and machine-readable knowledge sources.

Without a maintainable source model, these outputs may remain isolated project artefacts, become outdated or be recreated inconsistently across teams and channels.

## Proposed capability

The proposed approach would provide a structured source layer for selected reusable knowledge assets.

Approved source material could be adapted or published for use in:

- SharePoint;
- Confluence;
- internal newsletters;
- customer websites;
- applications;
- CRM and knowledge environments;
- service-design and project documentation; and
- emerging AI-enabled services.

The source layer would not replace these platforms.

Each platform would continue to perform its appropriate organisational, delivery, collaboration or publishing role.

Bitbucket would support selected reusable source material through:

- structured Markdown;
- visible ownership;
- version history;
- controlled contribution;
- peer review;
- approval records;
- content status;
- traceable decisions;
- reusable components; and
- machine-readable structure.

## Why Bitbucket

Bitbucket is an existing enterprise Git capability that has been used by City of Melbourne development teams for years.

The proposal does not require introducing an untested technology platform.

It explores whether an established capability can be used more broadly for selected documentation, design and customer-information assets.

Relevant existing Git capabilities include:

- version control;
- access management;
- branch-based change workflows;
- peer review through pull requests;
- change history;
- comparison between versions;
- approval controls; and
- integration opportunities.

The pilot must still test whether the contributor experience is practical for non-developers.
