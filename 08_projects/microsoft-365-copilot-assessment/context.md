# Microsoft 365 Copilot Capability Assessment — Context

## Background

Strategic OS uses a cloud-first operating model.

GitHub remains the authoritative Strategic OS source of truth.

Organisational platforms remain the systems of record for organisational content, governed data, communication, delivery activity and official records.

Microsoft 365 Copilot is being assessed as an enterprise AI layer for authorised organisational work.

The assessment must determine what is actually available in the organisation rather than assuming standard Microsoft functionality.

## Problem framing

Microsoft 365 Copilot may create useful operational leverage across meetings, organisational knowledge retrieval, communication and workflow automation.

However, actual usefulness depends on:

- licence;
- tenant configuration;
- permissions;
- administrator controls;
- organisational governance;
- privacy and records requirements;
- device and browser access;
- source quality;
- reliability;
- verification burden; and
- ongoing management effort.

The assessment therefore focuses on current tenant reality and operational value.

## Objective

Identify the smallest set of Microsoft 365 Copilot capabilities that can improve day-to-day work while requiring minimal recurring human administration.

## Assessment principles

### Decision First

Assess capabilities in terms of the decisions they enable and the recurring work they remove.

### Human in the Loop

Copilot may retrieve, summarise, draft, recommend or automate approved steps.

Humans remain responsible for judgement, validation and material decisions.

### Privacy by Design

Do not copy raw organisational source material into Strategic OS.

### Source-aware use

Distinguish:

- source evidence;
- assumption;
- interpretation;
- recommendation; and
- action.

### Low management overhead

A capability should not be adopted merely because it works.

It should remove more recurring administration than it introduces.

## Assessment scope

### In scope

- Teams meeting support
- SharePoint retrieval
- OneDrive retrieval
- Teams knowledge retrieval
- email retrieval and synthesis
- calendar support
- document support
- cross-source knowledge retrieval
- Copilot Search
- agents
- Copilot Studio
- Power Automate
- workflow automation
- low-risk administrative automation
- permissions
- governance
- licence and administrator dependencies
- browser and device constraints
- ongoing management burden

### Out of scope

- storing raw organisational evidence in Strategic OS;
- redesigning Microsoft 365 governance;
- building production automations before capability and governance are understood;
- assuming licence or tenant capability from public product documentation alone; and
- introducing new Strategic OS architecture unless repeated use exposes a genuine gap.

## Status vocabulary

Use one or more of:

- Confirmed
- Available but not enabled
- Unknown
- Restricted
- Requires licence
- Requires admin approval
- Requires user testing

## Decision outcomes

Use:

- Adopt
- Adopt with verification
- Pilot
- Defer
- Do not rely on
- Reject

## Evidence handling

Do not store:

- meeting transcripts;
- internal emails;
- organisational documents;
- customer or staff personal information;
- unredacted screenshots;
- confidential extracts;
- credentials;
- access tokens;
- detailed tenant configuration that should remain controlled; or
- sensitive operational evidence.

Strategic OS may retain:

- evidence status;
- safe source references;
- generalised observations;
- capability conclusions;
- caveats;
- decision logic;
- management-burden observations; and
- reusable lessons.

## Strategic OS integration hypothesis

Potential Microsoft 365 Copilot role:

- retrieve authorised organisational context;
- summarise organisational information;
- support meeting preparation and follow-through;
- support communication and document work;
- support approved workflow automation; and
- reduce repetitive administrative effort.

Potential Strategic OS role:

- preserve reusable interpretation;
- record capability decisions and caveats;
- retain operating patterns and lessons;
- support strategic reasoning; and
- avoid duplicating organisational source material.

This relationship must be validated through real use.

## Open questions

- Which Microsoft 365 Copilot licence is assigned?
- Which Copilot experiences are enabled?
- Is Teams transcription available and permitted?
- Is intelligent meeting recap available?
- Can Copilot retrieve authorised information across SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams and email?
- Is Copilot Search enabled?
- Are organisational agents available?
- Is Copilot Studio available?
- Is Power Automate available and appropriately integrated?
- Which connectors and workflow capabilities are approved?
- What administrator controls apply?
- What organisational AI, privacy, records and security guidance applies?
- What browser or device restrictions affect use?
- Which capabilities reduce recurring work enough to justify adoption?
