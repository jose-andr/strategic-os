# Microsoft 365 Copilot Capability Assessment — Stakeholder Notes

## Purpose

Capture safe, generalised stakeholder considerations relevant to adoption, governance and operational use.

Do not store sensitive stakeholder commentary, personal information or attributed private opinions.

## Stakeholder groups

### User

Primary interest:

- reduce recurring administration;
- improve access to organisational knowledge;
- improve meeting preparation and follow-through;
- preserve decision quality;
- avoid unnecessary tool maintenance.

Key question:

> Does this capability materially improve day-to-day work without adding disproportionate verification or management overhead?

### Information technology / Microsoft 365 administration

Likely interests:

- licensing;
- tenant configuration;
- access controls;
- supported applications;
- approved connectors;
- environment management;
- supportability.

Assessment need:

Confirm what is technically available and what requires administrator enablement or approval.

### Information security

Likely interests:

- access boundaries;
- data exposure;
- connector risk;
- agent behaviour;
- automation controls;
- external services.

Assessment need:

Confirm whether planned use remains within approved security boundaries.

### Privacy

Likely interests:

- meeting capture;
- personal information;
- automated processing;
- appropriate use;
- data handling;
- consent where relevant.

Assessment need:

Confirm whether meeting and knowledge use cases require specific privacy controls or guidance.

### Records and information management

Likely interests:

- source-of-truth ownership;
- record retention;
- meeting transcripts;
- generated outputs;
- duplication;
- authoritative records.

Assessment need:

Clarify which artefacts remain organisational records and prevent Strategic OS from becoming a duplicate records repository.

### Service or business owners

Likely interests:

- accuracy;
- currency;
- source quality;
- decision reliability;
- practical usefulness.

Assessment need:

Use known-answer tests to determine whether Copilot retrieves the right source and whether verification remains proportionate.

## Engagement approach

Engage stakeholders only when a real capability test exposes a dependency, risk or decision that cannot be resolved through normal approved use.

Avoid broad governance discovery before there is a concrete use case.

Prefer:

1. test a low-risk capability;
2. record the observable constraint;
3. identify the specific governance or administrator question;
4. seek targeted clarification; and
5. update the assessment decision.

## Current stakeholder status

No stakeholder conclusion is treated as confirmed until supported by organisational guidance or direct evidence.

## Open stakeholder questions

- Who can confirm the assigned Microsoft 365 Copilot licence?
- Who can confirm Teams transcription and recap policy?
- Who owns guidance for organisational use of Copilot agents?
- Who approves Power Automate connectors or workflow automation?
- Where is current organisational AI-use guidance maintained?
- What privacy or records rules apply to meeting recap and transcripts?
- Are there additional restrictions on browser-based Copilot access?
