## Publishing and governance workflow

The pilot would test a simple end-to-end workflow.

    Create or update source
            |
            v
    Draft change in Bitbucket
            |
            v
    Subject-matter review
            |
            v
    Design, product or technical review
            |
            v
    Asset-owner approval
            |
            v
    Merge approved source
            |
            v
    Adapt or publish to relevant channels
            |
            v
    Record channel publication and review date
            |
            v
    Maintain, supersede or retire

The workflow should make clear:

- who owns the source asset;
- who may propose changes;
- which reviewers are required;
- what evidence supports the change;
- who has approval authority;
- which channels are affected;
- who publishes into each channel;
- when the source must be reviewed;
- how previous versions are retained; and
- how outdated content is superseded or retired.
