# Conversation Strategic Signal

## Purpose

Convert an approved conversation, meeting or voice capture into a reviewed strategic signal that can support better decisions, stakeholder alignment and reusable learning.

This template is for abstraction and interpretation.

It must not be used to store raw recordings, full transcripts, customer records, sensitive organisational extracts or identifiable case details.

## When to use

Use this template when:

- an approved conversation contains a potentially important decision or unresolved question;
- stakeholder priorities, concerns or alignment signals have emerged;
- a commitment, risk or strategic opportunity should not be lost;
- a meeting or discussion contains reusable learning;
- a voice reflection may contain a useful Strategic OS signal; or
- an approved capture tool such as PLAUD has produced source material that needs human review before entering Strategic OS.

Do not create a retained Strategic OS artefact where the conversation contains no reusable strategic value.

## Privacy and retention review

Before retaining any output:

- [ ] Recording or capture was permitted for the context.
- [ ] Raw audio is not being stored in Strategic OS.
- [ ] Full transcript is not being stored in Strategic OS.
- [ ] Customer-identifiable information has been removed.
- [ ] Sensitive organisational detail has been removed or safely abstracted.
- [ ] Raw organisational source data has not been copied into the repository.
- [ ] Claims requiring validation against a system of record are clearly labelled.
- [ ] Only the minimum reusable strategic abstraction is being retained.
- [ ] The retained content is appropriate for an employer-independent personal strategic operating system.

## Human review checklist

Before treating this as Strategic OS knowledge:

- [ ] Evidence is separated from interpretation.
- [ ] Assumptions are visible.
- [ ] Caveats are recorded.
- [ ] Decisions are not inferred where none were made.
- [ ] Stakeholder intent is not over-interpreted.
- [ ] Commitments reflect what was actually agreed.
- [ ] The recommended action is proportionate.
- [ ] The output contributes to decision clarity, stakeholder alignment or reusable strategic capability.
- [ ] The destination is appropriate.
- [ ] Anything with no enduring value has been discarded.
